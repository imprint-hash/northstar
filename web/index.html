    How Northstar decides

    Lock

    The buyer funds the escrow

    USDC goes into a Solana program account tied to one ship, one port and a deadline. The program can pay only the supplier named at opening, or refund the buyer after the deadline. Nobody else can move it, Northstar included.

    Check

    Telegraph's miners answer, for a fee

    Before the voyage: company registry and sanctions screening. During it: the ship's live position from Quay and the port's congestion. Every answer is a paid machine-to-machine purchase with a Telegraph receipt.

    Settle

    Two fixes in port, then pay

    The ship must be stopped inside the destination port area on two fresh fixes at least 30 minutes apart. Then Northstar's verifier signs the release, with a hash of the receipts as evidence. A ship that goes dark or never arrives: the money stays put, then goes home.
